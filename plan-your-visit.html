Before You Go

Travel essentials that apply across all three districts.

Pack Right

  • Light woollens / jacket
  • Raincoat or umbrella
  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Basic medicines & first aid

Money & Network

  • Carry cash — ATMs are limited
  • Jio & Airtel work best; BSNL in parts
  • Network is limited in remote valleys

On the Road

  • Travel during daylight hours
  • Prefer local guidance for stays & treks
  • Check road conditions before travelling in monsoon (Jun–Sep)
  • Start early for day trips to Noney from Imphal
Where to Base Yourself

Stay options by district

Tamenglong

Assam Rifles Guest Room, Circuit House, Teacher's Guest House — plus Apollo Inn, Peace Hotel, Bg Hotel and JK Hotel in town.

Senapati

Homestays and eco-lodges including Dzukou Retreat, 3D Hotel, Susan Residencia Lodge and Lnc Hotel.

Noney

Accommodation is limited — base yourself in Imphal and take day trips to Khoupum Valley and surrounding attractions.

Festival Calendar

Time your visit around the hills' biggest celebrations.

Feb

Lui-Ngai-Ni across all three districts — seed-sowing festival of the Naga communities.

Dec – Jan

Orange Festival (Tamenglong) & Gaan-Ngai (Zeliangrong communities).

Dec 25

Christmas — widely celebrated with church services and community feasts across all districts.

Varies

Barak Festival (Senapati) and Chaga Ngee (Tamenglong) — check locally for exact dates.
